## 新增参数

| 前一个参数/新参数分组 | 参数名称 | 默认值 | 可选值 | 描述 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| --flashinfer-mxfp4-moe-precision | --flashinfer-allreduce-fusion-backend | None | auto, trtllm, mnnvl | Enable FlashInfer allreduce fusion and choose backend. Defaults to auto. 'auto': choose mnnvl on SM90 single-node systems and SM100/SM103 single-node or multi-node systems; choose trtllm otherwise. 'trtllm': available on single-node systems only. 'mnnvl': available on SM90 single-node systems and SM100/SM103 single-node or multi-node systems via MNNVL fabric. Fuses allreduce with Residual + RMSNorm for supported MoE models. |
| --max-mamba-cache-size | --enable-int8-mamba-checkpoint | False | bool flag (set to enable) | Store radix-cached linear-attn (mamba) states in int8 (separate checkpoint pool) for ~2x cached-prefix capacity at fixed memory. |
| --enable-int8-mamba-checkpoint | --int8-mamba-ckpt-size | None（运行时默认：2x 活跃池大小） | Type: int | Number of int8 mamba checkpoint slots (default: 2x the active mamba pool size). |
