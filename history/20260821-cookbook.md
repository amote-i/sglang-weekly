# SGLang Weekly Changelog — 2026-08-21 (Cookbook Supplement)

Supplement: newly supported models that were added as `docs/cookbook/` pages but
never appeared in the `docs/docs/supported-models/*.mdx` tables, and therefore
were not captured by earlier weekly reports. Covers the full tracking window of
this repository: `acd689b407a9fd42fbce4b40b04c304d9ba6adb7..0db2c53deca12715d209ffbd46bdfafdfd5a5d35`.

Excluded (not newly supported):

- DiffusionGemma, EmbeddingGemma, PaddleOCR-VL — already reported in earlier
  weekly files (20260612.md, 20260803.md, 20260821.md).
- LFM2.5, Laguna-M.1, Laguna-S-2.1, Laguna-XS-2.1 — model family already listed
  in the supported-models tables at the window base commit; their cookbook pages
  are documentation catch-up.

Model types for cookbook-only categories (diffusion image/video/world models,
VLA policies) do not fit the 6 standard supported-models types; they use the
taxonomy of the cookbook's own navigation (`docs/docs.json` and
`docs/cookbook/diffusion/intro.mdx`).
